High Voltage Power Supply from Recycled Parts

Nanomaterials Lab, Universidad Autónoma del Estado de Morelos · Advisor: Prof. Vivechana Agarwal · July – September 2021

The problem

Commercial equipment for both electrospinning and dip-coating is expensive enough to be the limiting factor on whether a lab can run the process at all. Thus, I, alongside my mentors, developed an electrospinning device using parts that can be acquired from a dumpster.

What I built

  • A High-Voltage Power Supply using a Cockroft-Walton Voltage Multiplier Circuit, a large capacitor that can be harvested from an air conditioner and an ignition coil that can be harvested from a car.
  • The aforementioned High-Voltage Power Supply was used to power an electrospinning device, although its high voltages could be used in ozone generators or X-ray machines.
  • I did not build this, but the paper also discussed the creation of a dip-coating device using parts harvested from a printer.

Output

  • Kumar Agarwal, S. S., Mendoza Enríquez, B. U., & Hernández Rivera, D. (2022). Device Fabrication from Recycled Electronic Spare Parts: Dip Coating Device and High Voltage Power Supply Adapted for Electrospinning Device. Journal of Physics: Conference Series, 2261, 012001. DOI
  • Best Presenter, 2022 International Conference on Information and Electronics Engineering.
