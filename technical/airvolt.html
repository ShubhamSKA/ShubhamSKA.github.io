AirVolt — Wireless Voltage Sensing

Wireless Sensing Circuits and Systems Lab, Purdue University · Advisor: Prof. Byunghoo Jung · August 2025 – May 2026

The problem

Measuring voltage on high-voltage AC equipment in a power plant, or on a power rail during installation and maintenance, normally requires contact. Contact requires the line to be de-energised, which means a shutdown just to take a reading.

My work

  • Leading early-stage development of the wireless sensing system.
  • Outlining the circuit architecture.
  • Designing the initial PCB prototypes in Altium Designer for proof-of-concept testing.

To expand — sensing principle, front-end architecture, board revisions, and proof-of-concept measurements.
