Magnetically Actuated Haptic Displays

Versatile Electronic Systems Lab, Cornell University · Advisor: Prof. Amal El-Ghazaly · September 2026 – Present

The problem

A haptic display has to hold a shape long enough for a finger to read it. Most soft actuators hold their shape only while current keeps flowing, which sets the power budget by how long the display stays up rather than by how often it changes.

The approach

Electro-permanent magnets switch between two stable magnetic states with a current pulse and then hold that state with no standing current. Built into a soft actuator, that decouples holding a shape from powering the display.

My work is on the actuator's form factor — the geometries and material stacks that turn the magnetic switching into useful displacement at a scale a fingertip can resolve.

To expand — actuator geometries under test, materials, drive electronics, and displacement and force measurements.
