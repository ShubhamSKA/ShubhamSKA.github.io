Machine Learning on Limited Materials Datasets

Nanomaterials Lab, Universidad Autónoma del Estado de Morelos · Advisor: Prof. Vivechana Agarwal · May 2026 – August 2026

The problem

It has been proven in a prior publication that machine learning can be used to determine contaminant, and on some occasions, concentration of the contaminant. However, these measurements are all taken at the same time. What if we want to determine these two parameters regardless of the time?

The approach

Using the approximately 600 point dataset with photoluminescense and colorimetry data, I created a two-model pipeline to determine the contaminant, and then, determine the concentration of said contaminant, regardless of the time at which the reaction was initialized relative to the measurement.

Results

Contaminant classification 97% accuracy
Concentration prediction 0.061 mean absolute error
Status Manuscript in progress. Kumar Agarwal, Avalos, et al.
