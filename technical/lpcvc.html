Low Power Computer Vision Challenge 2024

Vertically Integrated Projects, CVES, Purdue University · Advisor: Prof. Yung-Hsiang Lu · August 2023 – May 2024

The problem

An international challenge needs to rank submissions from many teams on both accuracy and power, fairly and reproducibly. Without a shared baseline, entrants cannot tell whether a submission is worth making. The competition that I was helping organize had the goal of finding the best model for 3D reconstruction in foggy environments on edge.

What I built

  • I was on the team from 2023-2024, as mentioned above, where I worked on trying to run open source 3D reconstruction models, and compare their performances. These would then be used for 3D reconstruction evaluation pipeline for the 2024 challenge. Unfortunately, the 2024 competition was not held... but, to my understanding, the infrastructure and models I left behind were used in the 2025 competition.
