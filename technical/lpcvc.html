Low Power Computer Vision Challenge 2024

Vertically Integrated Projects, CVES, Purdue University · Advisor: Prof. Yung-Hsiang Lu · August 2023 – May 2024

The problem

An international challenge needs to rank submissions from many teams on both accuracy and power, fairly and reproducibly. Without a shared baseline, entrants cannot tell whether a submission is worth making.

What I built

  • The 3D reconstruction evaluation pipeline for the 2024 challenge.
  • Benchmarks of open-source models, defining the power and accuracy baselines that competitor submissions were measured against.
