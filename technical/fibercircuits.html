FiberCircuits: System-on-Fiber

Responsive Environments, MIT Media Lab · Advisor: Prof. Joseph A. Paradiso · May 2026 – Present

The problem

Back in 2025, Cedric Honnet and a few other people published the first version of FiberCircuits, which was a 1.4mm fiber with a variety of sensors. Now, in 2026, I am colaborating with Cedric to create the next version of this. A sub-mm version of Fibercircuits, using the smallest existing microntroller, the MSPM0C1104 in its WLCSP-8 package.

The approach

This has entailed a variety of explorations:

  • The original FiberCircuits was coated in silicone to increase it's durability. However, this came with the tradeoff of inaccesbility to the PCB itself, restricting certain direct-contact interactions. So, for a couple of months, I explored different combinations of low cost materials that could serve as a replacement electrode. While I did not reach a satisfactory result, I did end up finding a piezoresistive composite, that could be used in future sensing applications.
  • Moving from 1.4mm to 1mm, brings in a surprising number of challenges, the first of which is component selection. I now have an extensive list of sub-mm sensors that I can refer to when attempting to explore new capabilities to append to the design.
  • I have also been exploring a variety of circuit topologies and applications, ranging from sleep sensing to electrostimulation, all on a fiber form factor.
