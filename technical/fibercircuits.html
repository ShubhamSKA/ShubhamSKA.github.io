FiberCircuits — System-on-Fiber

Responsive Environments, MIT Media Lab · Advisor: Prof. Joseph A. Paradiso · May 2026 – Present

The problem

Electronics woven into a textile stop reading as textile once the fiber is thick enough to feel. Width is the constraint that decides whether a sensing fiber can be worn all night without being noticed — which is the whole point for sleep applications.

The approach

I am building the next version of the FiberCircuits topology, taking the fiber from 1.4 mm down to under 1 mm while keeping it a complete system rather than a passive thread: components and interconnect topologies that support sleep detection and electrostimulation.

To expand — the topology itself, component selection and packaging at sub-millimetre width, the fabrication process, and what the fiber measures.
