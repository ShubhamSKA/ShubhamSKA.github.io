EdemaFlex: Hand Edema Treatment Glove

Hybrid Body Lab, Cornell University · Advisor: Prof. Cindy Hsin-Liu Kao · August 2026 – Present

The problem

Compression therapy for hand edema generally means a garment fitted and applied by someone else. A patient with reduced hand function is exactly the person least able to put on a tight glove unaided.

The approach

EdemaFlex is a knit glove with shape memory alloy springs worked into the structure. The springs contract on heating, so the garment applies its own compression rather than relying on a tight fit, and can be put on slack. It can also be adapted to specific users, as opposed to relying on a one-size fits all approach. This is especially necessary in hand edema treatment, where the swollenness of the hands can drastically alter their shape.

My work

  • Manufacturing the aforementioned gloves, as indicated by the existing methodology, while also trying to explore potential improvements to the design.
  • Updating the entire backend workflow to streamline the process in which gloves can be manufactured and troubleshot.
