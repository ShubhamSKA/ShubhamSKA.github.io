About Me

Hello! My name is Shubham. I'm a motivated, driven and innovative Computer Engineer with a degree from Purdue University, and now pursuing a Master of Engineering from Cornell University.
Throughout my academic journey, I have developed a strong interest in various aspects of technology, innovation, and programming. I am particularly enthusiastic about wearable technologies, firmware development, and human-technology interfaces. However, I am always eager to explore new technologies and expand my knowledge in these areas.
