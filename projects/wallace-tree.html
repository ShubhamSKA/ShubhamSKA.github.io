8-bit Wallace Tree Multiplier

ECE 45600, Purdue University · Cadence Virtuoso, 45 nm CMOS · April 2025

An 8 × 8 multiplier that forms all 64 partial products with AND gates at once, then squeezes them down with full adders (3:2 compressors), half adders (2:2) and buffers (1:1) across four sum layers, until every column holds at most two bits. A ripple-carry adder finishes the job. Fewer sequential addition stages means a shorter path from input to product.

Results

Transistors 2474, all minimum sized
Worst-case delay About 680 ps simulated, against 587.5 ps estimated
Average power 186.2 µW simulated, against 175.68 µW estimated
Structure Partial products, four sum layers, ripple-carry adder
