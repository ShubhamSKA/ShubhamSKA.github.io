Low Power Dynamic Flash ADC

Purdue University · Cadence Virtuoso, 45 nm CMOS

A 6-bit flash ADC compares the input against all 63 thresholds at once, which makes it fast and makes power the hard part. This design pairs a resistor ladder with dynamic StrongARM latch comparators, which only draw current while they switch, and a folding multiplexer encoder built from transmission gates and pass-transistor logic. It samples at 500 MS/s while averaging under 1 mW.

Results

Resolution 6 bits, rail to rail
Sample rate 500 MS/s; worst transition 1.07 ns, inside the 2 ns sample period
Average power 806.9 µW at 500 MS/s
Comparators StrongARM latches with SR latches; PMOS-tail below 0.5 V, NMOS-tail above
Ladder 30 Ω resistors, about 529 µW static
