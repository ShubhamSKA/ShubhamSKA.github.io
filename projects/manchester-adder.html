8-bit Manchester Carry Adder

ECE 45600, Purdue University · Cadence Virtuoso · April 2025

An adder is only as fast as its carry. A Manchester carry adder makes the carry chain as quick as possible: each bit works out whether it generates, propagates or deletes a carry, and those three signals drive the switches of a carry cell, so the carry itself only passes through one transmission gate per bit. This one is built in static logic, from the inverter, NAND, NOR and XOR gates up to the full 8-bit layout, DRC and LVS clean.

Results

Before layout Correct sums at 1 GHz; 16.85 µW (I×V) or 18.17 µW (device power)
After layout One transition too slow at 1 GHz, correct at 500 MHz; 80.26 µW or 83.05 µW
Verification DRC and LVS clean for the 1-bit cell and the full 8-bit adder
